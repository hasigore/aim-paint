Auxiliary Hold

Temporary releases

These releases can happen anywhere during the combo.

The mandatory release at the end of a combo is separate and does not count toward this counter.

After a successful combo, the first sequence button becomes RED. The auxiliary button must be released during that same red window.

After the red window ends, the first sequence button becomes GREEN. Press it first, then press the auxiliary button within its press window.
Follow the sequence exactly.
Red = wait — the next button cannot be pressed yet.
Green = press the indicated button.
Hold = release the pressed button before the timer expires.

Example: SPACE → RMB → SPACE
SPACE → RMB RED → RMB GREEN → RMB → SPACE RED → SPACE GREEN.

After the final SPACE: first SPACE becomes RED and LSHIFT must be released during that same red window.
Then: first SPACE becomes GREEN → press SPACE → LSHIFT becomes required → press LSHIFT.
